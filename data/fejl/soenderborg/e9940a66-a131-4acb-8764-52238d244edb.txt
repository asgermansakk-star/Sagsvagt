Beskrivelse af aktiviteten eller initiativet - hvad er formålet med aktiviteten eller initiativet?
Intro Team is hosting a Christmas Craft event at Mejeriet for students and local residents.
We’ll provide the materials for making Christmas decorations, along with a cozy atmosphere,
festive music, and free hot drinks.
Hvorfor skal Sønderborg Ungdomsråd støtte lige præcis dette, og på hvilken måde gavner
dette unge mellem 15 og 25 år i Sønderborg?
The Christmas Craft event is a wonderful opportunity for students to connect with local
residents and with each other in a relaxed and welcoming setting. By creating their own
Christmas decorations, participants and especially students can bring a bit of warmth and
holiday spirit into their homes, which helps during this dark and often stressful time of the
year. The event also encourages creativity, gives everyone a break from their daily routines,
and helps build a sense of community across cultures and backgrounds.
